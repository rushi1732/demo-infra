Our History

29 Years of Engineering Mastery

Founded in 1997 with a pioneering vision to transform Pune's real estate landscape, SR Developer began as an ambitious infrastructure contracting enterprise. Over nearly three decades of relentless commitment, the conglomerate has grown exponentially into Western India's premier development powerhouse.

1997 - 2005

Foundational Engineering & Contracting

Mastered precision concrete civil contracting for state infrastructure, bridges, and industrial layouts. Established the core corporate reputation for uncompromising structural integrity and zero debt exposure.

2006 - 2016

Scale, Integrated Townships & Hospitality

Expanded into integrated residential gated communities and landmark leisure projects including The Corinthians Resort & Club. Pioneered early adoption of earthquake-resistant structural standards in South Pune.

2017 - Present

German MIVAN Precision & Grade-A IT Parks

Commissioned SR Unitree corporate tech park in Yerwada and ultra-luxury residential towers like SR Emerald and Esteban II. 100% MahaRERA statutory escrow compliance and delivered 40M+ sq. ft. of built space.

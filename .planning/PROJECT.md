# Nyati Group Luxury Real Estate & Diversified Conglomerate Web Experience

## Overview
A high-fidelity, ultra-premium recreation and elevation of the official Nyati Group portal (https://nyatigroup.com/). Nyati Group is one of Pune and Western India's most prestigious real estate developers and infrastructure conglomerates, established in 1997 with over 40 million sq. ft. delivered, 70+ landmark projects, and diversified presence across Luxury Real Estate, Nyati EPC, Hospitality (The Corinthians Resort & Club), NL Healthcare, and Swastik Wellbeing.

## Brand Identity & Aesthetic Direction
- **Typography:** Cinzel / Cinzel Decorative (Headings & Architectural accents), Plus Jakarta Sans / Inter (Crisp body & specs).
- **Color Palette:**
  - Deep Obsidian / Charcoal (`#0a0d12`, `#12161f`)
  - Warm Architectural Stone / Alabaster (`#f7f6f2`, `#ffffff`)
  - Royal Muted Bronze & Antique Gold (`#c5a880`, `#b38e5d`, `#917246`)
  - Slate & Muted Silver borders (`#2a303c`, `#e5e2db`)
- **Visuals:** Immersive video hero background, full-screen architectural photography, responsive project filter tabs, interactive floor plans & master layout viewer, project inquiry modal, amenities showcase, RERA compliance badges, and corporate vertical transitions.
- **Iconography:** Consistent Lucide SVG icons (no emoji UI icons).

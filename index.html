Who We Are

A Legacy Built On Concrete Trust & Innovation

The Nyati Group has been at the forefront of Pune’s Real Estate Scenario since 1997. The brand stands tall amongst the most respected and admired real estate conglomerates in India, shaping modern lifestyles with acute focus on architecture, structural longevity, and customer-first values.

Under the visionary stewardship of Chairman & Managing Director Dr. Nitin Nyati, the group has diversified across premium residential towers, IT business hubs, nationwide infrastructure EPC contracts, luxury hospitality, and holistic wellbeing.

  • 100% On-Time Delivery: Track record of delivering residential enclaves on or ahead of schedule.
  • Advanced German Formwork Construction: Engineered with shear wall technology for seismic safety and superior thermal insulation.
  • Holistic Community Living: Integrated clubhouses, olympic-length swimming pools, green oxygen zones, and 24/7 smart security.

Vertical 01

High-Rise Residential Construction

We engineer high-rise residential landmarks utilizing cutting-edge German monolithic aluminum formwork (MIVAN technology). Every wall and slab is poured in a continuous monolithic cycle, eliminating brick joints and creating impervious, seismic-resilient structural shells.

01

Monolithic MIVAN Formwork

Continuous casting guarantees 3x higher strength, zero seepage risk, and lightning-fast slab cycle delivery.

02

Fe550D TMT & M40 Structural Concrete

Certified corrosion-resistant steel rebar and high-grade ready-mix concrete batching with digital slump monitoring.

03

Acoustic Glazing & Thermal Comfort

Double-glazed soundproof facade systems and green reflective tinting reduce interior energy cooling demand by up to 28%.

Vertical 02

Civil & Heavy EPC Infrastructure

SR Developer's civil engineering division delivers vital urban infrastructure connecting communities. From multi-lane reinforced concrete road corridors and flyovers to comprehensive utility lifelines.

01

Arterial Road Networks & Grade Separators

Heavy-duty PQC pavement, pre-stressed girder bridges, and signal-free traffic corridors designed for 50-year design life.

02

Smart Utility Ducting & Stormwater EPC

Underground optical fiber conduits, concealed power trunking, and high-discharge box drain networks preventing seasonal waterlogging.

03

Zero-Discharge STP & Water Harvesting

Automated sequential batch reactor (SBR) sewage treatment recycling 100% of greywater for landscaping and dual flushing.
